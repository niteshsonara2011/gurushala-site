Website policy

Accessibility

Updated 5 September 2026

Gurushala wants its knowledge to be usable by as many people as possible, including people using phones, assistive technology or larger text.

What we are building toward

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and visible focus states.
  • Readable contrast, scalable text and responsive mobile layouts.
  • Meaningful image descriptions where images communicate information.
  • Gujarati, Hindi and English access where practical.
  • Clear language and alternatives for important information.
We are not claiming full accessibility conformance yet. The website is in public soft launch and is being tested continuously.

Report a problem

If a page, document or control is difficult to use, email info@gurushala.org and tell us which page you were using and what assistance would help.
