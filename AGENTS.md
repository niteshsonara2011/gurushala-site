# Gurushala Website Developer Instructions

## Current identity

- **Legal entity:** Valmiki Gurushala Trust
- **Official website:** gurushala.org
- **Trust location:** Gandhinagar, Gujarat, Bharat
- **Regional work context:** Kapadvanj and surrounding communities
- Gurushala is women-led and community-rooted.
- Do not present Geyvak, Reva, Nitesh, or any external technical supporter as founder, owner, trustee, director, publisher, or public face unless formally approved.

## Public language and tone

- Default website language: Gujarati.
- Hindi and English may be offered as additional languages.
- Keep Gujarati simple, readable and village-friendly.
- Use a dignified, practical, warm tone.
- Avoid pity-based, caste-exploitative, political, savior-style or exaggerated claims.

## Publishing identity

- Publisher: **Gurushala Publication**.
- Geyvak may be described only as a private market/distribution partner when explicitly relevant.
- Do not present Geyvak as publisher.
- First announced book: **Ushaniruddha: Journey of Usha to Aniruddha**, publication November 2026.
- Do not publicly promote the Utkanteshwar history project until physical research has been completed and approved for publication.

## Website goals

The public website should clearly communicate:

1. Gurushala's vision and community purpose
2. Women-led governance and dignity
3. Education, technology, knowledge and cultural work
4. Current initiatives with truthful status labels
5. Publications and learning resources
6. Trust registration and transparency information
7. Contact and participation information

## Design principles

- Mobile-first and readable on low-cost phones.
- Lightweight pages that remain usable on slower internet.
- High contrast, generous spacing and readable Gujarati typography.
- Calm, grounded, contemporary visual direction.
- Prefer local, truthful visual assets.
- Do not use tiny image sprites enlarged into programme photography.
- Avoid patch-on-patch CSS. Keep active production styles simple and consolidated.
- Important public copy belongs in HTML, not CSS-generated `content:` text.

## Safety and truthfulness

- Do not invent registration numbers, directors, addresses, approvals, funding, partnerships or impact claims.
- Do not publish sensitive personal information.
- Do not add individual names unless explicitly approved.
- Preserve legal and governance wording unless an approved correction is provided.

## Development rules

- Preserve the working deployment.
- Make reviewable changes and keep code maintainable.
- Prefer removing obsolete code over layering additional overrides.
- Check mobile layouts after homepage changes.
- Check Gujarati, Hindi and English language switching after copy changes.
- Confirm that deleted files are no longer referenced before removing them.
- Summarize changed files after each task.
