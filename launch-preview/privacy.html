Website policy

Privacy

Valmiki Gurushala Trust · Public soft launch · Updated 5 September 2026

Gurushala is still building its digital systems. This notice describes the current website, not future services that have not yet launched.

What the website currently collects

gurushala.org does not currently provide account registration, online payments or a website contact form. If you email us, your message and the information you choose to provide will be handled through our email service.

Language preference

The homepage stores your selected language in your browser using local storage so the site can remember Gujarati, Hindi or English on your device.

External services

The website may load fonts and credited visual resources from external providers. Those providers may receive ordinary technical information such as your IP address and browser request when their files are loaded.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not intentionally publish personal information about community participants without an appropriate basis and consent.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to info@gurushala.org.
